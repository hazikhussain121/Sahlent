# Sahlent

A lightweight, ad-free Android app that automatically silences your phone during salah times.

**Built as Sadaqah Jariyah** 🤲

## Features

- 🔕 **Auto-Silent Mode** - Automatically enables Do Not Disturb during prayer times
- 🕐 **Prayer Times** - Accurate prayer times based on your location (powered by Aladhan API)
- 🧭 **Qibla Compass** - Find the direction of the Kaaba
- 📿 **Tasbih Counter** - Digital counter with haptic feedback

## Why Sahlent?

- ✅ 100% Free & Ad-Free
- ✅ No tracking or data collection
- ✅ Lightweight and battery-efficient
- ✅ Works offline (after initial sync)
- ✅ Beautiful, distraction-free Islamic design

## Permissions

- **Location** - To calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction
- **Do Not Disturb Access** - To silence your phone during salah
- **Internet** - To fetch prayer times from Aladhan API

## Building

1. Open project in Android Studio
2. Sync Gradle
3. Run on device/emulator
